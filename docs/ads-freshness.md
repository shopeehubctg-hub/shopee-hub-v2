# Advertising data freshness

The server-only `GET /api/admin/ads-freshness` endpoint shows a Supabase health snapshot to an active Super Admin. It uses the signed-in user's tenant, checks the newest `ad_performance_daily.performance_date`, the newest `synced_at`, and coverage against that tenant's `stores` list. Missing stores include their latest historical ad date, or `null` if they have no ad history. The endpoint does not expose credentials or metrics.

The expected business date is the day before the current Kuala Lumpur date after 17:20, and two days before it earlier in the day. This allows the afternoon `FullAd` import and the second Apps Script trigger to finish. The write check expects a database write by approximately 09:20 and 17:20; Google `nearMinute()` time triggers may run 15 minutes either side of the configured minute. These are operational thresholds, not guarantees that Shopee or BigSeller has finalized its data.

`businessDateStatus` reports `current`, `delayed`, `no_data`, or `future_date` when a row claims a business date after the current Kuala Lumpur date. `writeStatus` reports `recent`, `delayed`, or `no_data`. Coverage is separate: a current business date can still have missing stores. As of 2026-09-25, `Supu` (`shopee-supu`) has no `FullAd` source rows, so the known coverage is 42 of 43 Dashboard stores. Do not map unrelated stores to Supu or fabricate its metrics.

The bound Apps Script writes each run's result to the spreadsheet's `_SyncLog`. It records `failed` when the sync throws, and `warning` when stores are unmatched. The updated script also records `sourceLatestDate` in every `full` run detail. Supabase `synced_at` proves that a row was written; it does **not** prove that the whole Apps Script run succeeded. For a failure alert, use an operator-owned monitor after the late run (for example, 18:00 Kuala Lumpur time) to inspect both the health endpoint and `_SyncLog`, then notify for `failed`, delayed business dates/writes, `future_date`, or changed coverage. No alert recipient or delivery channel is configured in this repository.

The repository copy of `google-apps-script/Code.gs` does not deploy itself. In [Shopee Ads Report](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13NOwTGkbDjW8y869CvS6lr6H8I7XRn3I0urt_-rqkgs/edit), open **Extensions → Apps Script**, replace the bound `Code.gs` with the reviewed version, run `installDailyTrigger`, and confirm both 09:05 and 17:05 time triggers. Do not put the Supabase secret in a sheet cell. See `google-apps-script/SETUP.md` for verification steps.
